# EMPIRE-ONE HERITAGE

## Overview
EMPIRE-ONE HERITAGE is a digital archive and family tree platform dedicated to preserving, documenting, and celebrating our family history across generations.

## Key Features
- **Legacy & Genealogy**: Interactive visualization of family roots, branches, and ancestral history.
- **Family Achievements**: Showcasing milestones, strength, and success within the family unit.
- **Media Archives**: Centralized repository for family records, documents, and historical media.
